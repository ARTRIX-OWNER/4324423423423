What APPLEX AI TRADING does

How AI analysis works

Candle data is requested from the connected market-data provider and normalised. A confluence engine then evaluates trend direction, moving-average alignment, momentum and candle structure, and confirms the reading against stored historical candles. A setup is only presented when the configured quality and payout thresholds are met, together with a confidence score. AI chart analysis is a separate module: a submitted chart image is described back to the member as structured trend, structure and bias notes.

How results are calculated

Each published signal stores its asset, direction, timeframe and entry timestamp. The closing candle of the entry period decides the outcome: WIN, or a recovery (MTG/Gale) step win where the strategy uses one, a flat refund when the candle closes level, or LOSS. Win rate is calculated as WINS / (WINS + LOSSES) × 100. Stored outcomes are never edited, hidden or reclassified.

Frequently asked questions

What is APPLEX AI TRADING?

APPLEX AI TRADING is an AI-powered trading analysis platform designed to provide market analysis, signal intelligence, historical performance tracking and specialized trading tools through a modern command-center interface. Members sign in to a workspace of analysis modules; the platform itself does not place trades.

What is APPLEX AI?

APPLEX AI is the short brand name for APPLEX AI TRADING. Both names refer to the same platform published on the official APPLEX AI TRADING website.

Is APPLEX AI a broker?

APPLEX AI TRADING is a software platform. It is not a broker, exchange, fund manager or financial advisor, it does not hold client funds and it does not execute trades on behalf of users.

Does APPLEX AI guarantee profits?

No. No trading technology can guarantee profits. APPLEX AI TRADING publishes analysis and stored results, and market outcomes always involve risk of loss.

What tools does APPLEX AI provide?

Live Signal, AI Chart Analysis, Future Signal, Live Telegram Bot Signals, APPLEX Checker, Money Management, AI Voice Manager, Result Checker, Risk Manager, News Signal, Best Pairs, Signal History and APPLEX / COMBINER PRO. Tool availability depends on the access level assigned to each member account.

What is APPLEX AI COMBINER PRO?

COMBINER PRO is a market terminal that scans connected assets, applies a multi-indicator confluence strategy with a payout filter, and publishes a single qualified setup with direction, entry time, timeframe and confidence on an MT4-style live chart. Each published signal is stored and later resolved from the closing candle.

What is APPLEX AI Voice Manager?

Voice Manager is a voice-assisted session module. A member enters capital, target and stop loss, and the assistant speaks money-management steps, session status and trade results back to the member.

How does APPLEX AI signal analysis work?

Candle data is requested from the connected market-data provider, normalised, then evaluated by a confluence engine (trend direction, moving-average alignment, momentum, candle structure and historical backtest confirmation). A setup is only presented when the configured quality and payout thresholds are met, together with a confidence score.

What is the difference between Demo and Live data?

Demo areas — including the public Demo Dashboard and historical chart examples — use sample or past data and are labelled Demo / Historical Example. Live areas are connected to the market-data provider in real time and are labelled LIVE MARKET DATA. Demo values are never presented as real member performance.

How does APPLEX AI display trading results?

Each signal is stored with asset, direction, timeframe, signal timestamp, resolved timestamp and outcome: WIN, MTG/Gale step win (MTG 1 or MTG 2), flat refund, or LOSS. Records are immutable — a stored WIN is never changed to a LOSS and a stored LOSS is never changed to a WIN.

How is the win rate calculated?

WIN RATE = WINS / (WINS + LOSSES) x 100, counted from stored results only. MTG/Gale step wins count as wins because the trade recovered; flat candles are refunds and are excluded from the loss count.

Is the Demo Dashboard live?

No. The Demo Dashboard on the homepage is an interactive demo built from sample and historical example values so visitors can see the interface. Live market modules are available inside the member workspace.

Who should use APPLEX AI?

Traders who want structured market analysis, signal presentation, session money management and an auditable result history in one dashboard. It is not suitable for anyone who cannot accept the risk of losing capital.

What are the risks of trading?

Trading involves substantial risk and may result in financial loss. APPLEX AI TRADING provides analytical tools and information; it does not guarantee trading outcomes or profits. Users should independently evaluate market conditions and manage risk responsibly.

How do members get access?

A user registers, verifies their email, and waits for owner approval. The owner then assigns the account's access level, tool permissions and daily credits. Members cannot change their own permissions.
